Everything since v0.15.0:

- **Career mode** (M4-CAREER-01 to -03): the RECORDS entry on the title is
  now **CAREER**. Make up to three players, so a family or friends can share
  the cartridge:
  - Name them on an arcade keyboard and choose their look: body, skin,
    hair, shirt, hat, and paddle. The demo player on the court stops and
    wears it while you edit.
  - Each player keeps their own stats: wins and losses, aces, shutouts,
    best streak, and longest rally.
  - Playing unlocks hats and hair colours. The player card shows the next
    one and how to earn it, and a match that earns one ends with
    **UNLOCKED!**
  - Play as your player: Player Select starts on their look, their name is
    on the scoreboard, and open play uses them too. **NO PLAYER** plays
    without one, for a friend borrowing controller 1.
  - The old records table is the last row of the CAREER page.
- **Saves:** your records, settings, and initials carry over (v0.4.0 and
  later saves), in a new layout with room for three players.
- **Known issues:** Player Select doesn't show your player's hat, and the
  park's walk-around sprite keeps its own look (your games there use your
  player).
