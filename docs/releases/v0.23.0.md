Everything since v0.22.0:

- **A tidier title menu:** the ways to play come first (SINGLES, DOUBLES,
  OPEN PLAY, PRACTICE), then CAREER and RECORDS (now on the menu), then
  COURT, OPTIONS, and MODIFIERS. BALL SPEED is gone (FAST BALL is the
  speed setting), WIN BY 2 is OPTIONS' SCORING, and CAMERA is ON or OFF
  with its own AMOUNT. A career player picks only a paddle on Player
  Select, and their records take their name.
- **Who's who:** the title shows who's joined (1P to 4P), and in a match a
  ring at each player's feet shows who plays them: 1P red, 2P blue, CPUs
  grey. Press Start on another controller to join.
- **Stars that show up:** winning a long rally now really gets you a star
  (it used to vanish at once). A point shows one star, for the player who
  hit the winner, and one face, on the player who missed.
- **Smoother:** the new effects stay out of the busiest frames, so points
  and serves don't stutter.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
