Everything since v0.18.0:

- **The kitchen rule:** volleying from your own kitchen (the non-volley
  zone next to the net), or from its line, now loses the point:
  **KITCHEN!** You can still walk in the kitchen, and hit a ball there
  after it bounces. The CPUs know the rule and never volley from it.
- **Five new modifiers** on the MODIFIERS page. They mix freely with each
  other and with BIG BALL, FAST BALL, WIN BY 2, and LAVA:
  - **VISION: GHOST.** Up in the air, the ball only flickers into view.
    Its shadow never leaves: read the shadow.
  - **RALLY: POPCORN.** Every hit speeds the ball up a little, up to half
    again as fast, so long rallies get frantic. Each serve starts fresh.
  - **FOOTING: ICE.** Players take longer to get going and slide when they
    let go. Start early, and let go before you arrive.
  - **LIGHTS: GLOW.** Lights out: a night court with lime lines, and the
    players and the ball glow with neon green outlines, neon outfits and
    hats, and neon paddles.
  - **COLORS: INVERSE.** Every colour on the court inverted, like a photo
    negative. The scoreboard stays as it is.
- **Your save** moves to a new layout the first time you start this
  version. Your records, settings, and career players all carry over, and
  the new modifiers start off.
- Behind the scenes: a draft of the instruction booklet, and the plan for
  four players on a Super Multitap (not in this release).
