Everything since v0.4.0:

- **Sound** (ADR-029, ADR-031): paddle hits (sharper drives, softer lobs and
  dinks), bounces, the net, a referee's whistle for faults, crowd applause
  that grows with the rally, and menu sounds. Each venue has a quiet
  background: birds at the park, gulls and water by the lake, crickets at
  sunset. OPTIONS has SOUND: ALL / NO CROWD / OFF.
- **Practice mode** (ADR-028): a ball machine feeds drives, lobs, dinks, or a
  mix to the kitchen, deep, your forehand or backhand, at you, or anywhere.
  Your best return streak is saved.
- **Opponent cards** (ADR-026): intro lines, taunts, and concessions on the
  ladder. Beat the Champs to clear it. After a win, B goes straight on to
  the next opponent.
- **Two-tone courts** (ADR-027): the kitchen has its own colour, and every
  venue is two-toned by default.
- **Pause menu** (ADR-030): RESUME, RESTART, MAIN MENU in matches and
  practice.
- **KID** difficulty also serves for you.
- **Fixes:**
  - the title menu no longer flickers or loses items
  - steadier frame rate on the title and in doubles
  - the late whistle and applause
  - a stray intro-card state at power-on
- **Cartridge:** 512 KiB with 2 KiB of battery SRAM. Saves from v0.4.0 reset
  once (the save layout grew).
