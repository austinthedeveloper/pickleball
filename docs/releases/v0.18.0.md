Everything since v0.17.0:

- **CAMERA** (new, off by default): a CAMERA row on the OPTIONS page makes
  the court lean gently toward the ball as it moves, the way some older
  console sports games drift their camera. Choose OFF, or 50% to 100% in
  5% steps. Try it on the OPTIONS page: the demo behind the page shows the
  setting live.
- **Parallax** (with the camera on): the scenery moves in layers. The
  trees and stands behind the court move less than the court, and the
  fence in front moves more, so the court has depth.
- The camera and parallax change only what you see: play, the CPUs, and
  your saves are unchanged. With CAMERA OFF the game runs as before.
