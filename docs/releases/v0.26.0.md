Everything since v0.25.0:

- **Tutorial:** new to the game? Pick **TUTORIAL** on the title. The ball
  machine (and the pickle, cheering behind it) walks you through six
  short steps: swing, aim, drive, lob, dink, and slice. Three good
  returns of each shot move you on, and a miss never sets you back. The
  balls come slowly, straight at you. Finish and you get **WELL DONE!**
- **Four players:** plug a Super Multitap into port 2 and play doubles
  with up to four people: 1P and 3P against 2P and 4P, with a CPU in any
  empty spot. Each player's ring shows their colour: red, blue, yellow,
  or green.
- **Pick your partner:** at the park, a career player can press Y at the
  rack to pick any captain they've beaten on the ladder as their partner.
- **Hitting poses:** players now reach for a stretched ball, swing
  overhead at a high one, and scoop a low one, so you can see why a shot
  went wide.
- **The title menu** has TUTORIAL after PRACTICE; everything below it
  moved down one.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
