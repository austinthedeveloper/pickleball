Everything since v0.24.0:

- **Park points:** open play has stakes now. A career player starts with
  10 park points; win a game at your level and you earn 2, lose and you
  drop 1. The courts are **ROOKIE**, **CLUB**, and **CHAMP**: CLUB opens
  at 15 points and CHAMP at 25, and once a court is open it stays open.
  Your points show in the park, on the match result, and on your career
  card. Without a career player, or on KID, every court is open as before.
- **Winners stay on:** like a real park, the team that wins stays on the
  court and the next team in line comes on to challenge them, for up to
  two wins in a row. Win your game and you're called right back with the
  same partner: **WINNERS STAY!**
- **A fairer ladder:** every rung, in singles and doubles, is now clearly
  harder than the one before. The Goth Club, the Surf Crew, the Twins,
  and the Champs were too close together in doubles, and in singles Raven
  and Kai, and Max and Ace, were about as hard as each other. The Champs
  in doubles are a little more beatable than before.
- **Doubles plays the same every time:** the same moves now play out the
  same doubles match, however long you spent in the menus first.
- **A smoother finish:** winning a park game as a career player no longer
  hitches for a moment as the result appears.
- **Your save** carries over as it is, and your career players start with
  10 park points.
