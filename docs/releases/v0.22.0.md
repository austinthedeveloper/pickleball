Everything since v0.21.0:

- **A fairer ladder:** the opponents from the Snowbirds up were too hard
  to beat on MEDIUM. Every rung is easier now, with a steady climb from
  the Hendersons to the Champs, in singles and in doubles.
- **Short serves you can return:** the big hitters used to angle a fast
  serve just over the kitchen and off the court. A CPU's short serve is
  now soft and down the middle.
- **More to say:** every team has sixteen quips now (four before), and
  they come in a shuffled order, so you hear all of a team's lines before
  any comes back.
- **Players react:** after a point the winners celebrate their own way
  (the Hendersons give a thumbs-up, the Gym Bros flex, the Pirates draw
  their paddles), and the losers slump. Run for a ball that gets past you
  and you dive for it.
- **Faces and flourishes:** a quip comes with a face to match (`!` into
  the net, `?` for an out, a sweat drop for one you couldn't reach), a
  star for winning a long rally, a note for a dink rally, and Z's if you
  take your time to serve. The ball kicks up dust where it lands, shakes
  the net when it hits it, squashes and stretches on a drive, and leaves
  spin lines behind drives and slices.
- **Visitors:** a gull flies over the lake, a satellite drifts across
  space, and a fish swims by under the sea. Clear the ladder and you get
  a trophy; practice with the pickle mascot cheering you on.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
