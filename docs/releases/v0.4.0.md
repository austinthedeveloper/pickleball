First tagged release. Everything since the proof of concept (0.3.0):

- **Battery save** (ADR-021): records against the CPU on a RECORDS page,
  kept in 2 KiB of battery SRAM.
- **Court colours** (ADR-022): surface, surround, and line presets with a
  live preview on the COURT page.
- **CPU personalities** (ADR-023): eight opponents with their own looks and
  play styles on a saved ladder (the Hendersons through the Champs).
- **Venues** (ADR-024): PARK, LAKESIDE, and SUNSET; AUTO plays each
  opponent at their home court.
- **Options** (ADR-025): points to win (3-21) and KID / EASY / MEDIUM /
  HARD difficulty, with a slow ball and swing assist for KID.
- Earlier in 0.3.x: doubles, lob/dink/drive shots, Player Select with paddle
  stats, and the female variant.
