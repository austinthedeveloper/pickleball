Everything since v0.8.0:

- **The clubhouse** (M4-OPEN-05): the open play park is twice as wide
  and scrolls as you walk. Follow the path right to the clubhouse and go
  in through the door:
  - **B at the records board** reads your records, a page per press.
  - **B at the ball machine** starts practice on your PRACTICE settings;
    **PARK** on the pause menu brings you back.
  - The teams waiting for a court sit on the bench.
  - You can still be called for your game while you're inside.
- **Smarter aim** (ADR-039): in doubles, the smarter teams (and your CPU
  partner) hit at the opponent who is back when one is up at the net,
  into an open lane, or down the middle.
- **Fixes:** the doubles result screen no longer drops frames after a
  ladder match.
- **Saves:** unchanged from v0.8.0 (v0.4.0 and later saves carry over).
