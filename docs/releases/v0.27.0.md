Everything since v0.26.0:

- **The push:** aiming deep is now a skill. Hold the Control Pad toward
  the net as you hit, and the longer you hold it, the deeper a standard
  shot or drive goes. A tap lands mid-court, about half a second pins
  them on the baseline, and hold it a second or more and the ball sails
  out. Up or Down still aims at a sideline while you push. Lobs, dinks,
  slices, and serves work as before.
- **The CPU teams** play exactly as they did: they never push a shot out.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
