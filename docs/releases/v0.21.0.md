Everything since v0.20.0:

- **New player models:** every player is redrawn, with a proper paddle,
  a real windup and follow-through, and shading. Players now have real
  hair under their hats, so going hatless (NONE) finally looks like it.
- **Seven new hats** to earn in Career: a beanie, a bucket hat, a
  backwards cap, a propeller beanie, a viking helmet, a chef's hat, and a
  crown. They're for looks only. The visor, headband, tricorn, and
  bandana are redrawn too.
- **The Hat Museum:** press X on a career player's card to see every hat
  on your player in a display case. Locked hats are blacked out and show
  how to earn them; press B to put on one you've unlocked.
- **CPU serves have personality:** the hard hitters serve fast, and now
  and then one goes into the net.
- **Fixed:** the career card's unlock count now reads past 9 (it showed
  0/6).
- **Your save** carries over as it is: your hats, unlocks, and records
  are all still there.
