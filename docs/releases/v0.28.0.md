Everything since v0.27.0:

- **Story mode: save the park!** A notice on the court fence says the
  park is going to be paved into a parking lot. Mr. Grimsby makes you a
  deal: beat the Champs, and the courts stay. Pick **STORY** at the top of
  the title menu and team up with your new partner, **Pepper**, to take on
  all eight teams, one chapter each, on their home courts. Every chapter
  opens with a short scene (B turns the page, START skips it), and you'll
  find out who's really working for Grimsby. Your place in the story is
  saved, so you can stop anytime and pick up where you left off.
- **The title menu** has STORY first; the cursor still starts on SINGLES.
- **Your save** carries over with everything kept, and the story starts at
  chapter 1.
