Everything since v0.7.0: smarter opponents and a smarter CPU partner.

- **Net play** (ADR-035): from the Gym Bros on, CPUs volley once the two
  bounces are done and come up to the kitchen line. Later teams play the
  net more.
- **Doubles teamwork** (ADR-036): CPU pairs cover the middle, poach at the
  net, and shift with the ball. Your CPU partner comes in with you, stays
  back beside you, and never leaves its own half short.
- **Situational shots** (ADR-037): come to the net and the smarter teams
  (the Pirates on) answer with soft drops into your kitchen and dink
  rallies.
- **Doubles balance** (ADR-038): the top teams rarely mis-hit, and in
  doubles they hit down the middle, between you and your partner. The
  doubles ladder now gets harder rung by rung; the Twins are no longer
  easy if you play the net.
- **Your CPU partner** plays all of this on your side.
- **Saves:** unchanged from v0.7.0 (v0.4.0 and later saves carry over).
