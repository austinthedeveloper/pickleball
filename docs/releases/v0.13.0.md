Everything since v0.12.0:

- **Opponent hats** (M4-OPP-04): the ladder teams wear their own look: a
  visor for the Hendersons' DAD, the Snowbirds, and the Champs, red
  headbands for the Gym Bros, a pirate tricorn for PETE and a bandana for
  BILGE, and bare hair for the Goth Club and the Surf Crew.
- **Record initials** (M4-SAVE-03): beat your best win streak or the
  longest rally and the match ends with NEW RECORD!: enter three initials,
  arcade style. They show on the records page. A winning run that already
  holds the best streak raises it without asking again.
- **Saves:** your records and settings carry over (v0.4.0 and later saves),
  in a new layout with room for the initials. Existing records show `---`
  until someone beats them.
