Everything since v0.11.0:

- **Close call replay** (ADR-041): after a long rally, a ball that lands
  just out gets a close-up of the line: the ball dropping past it in slow
  motion, and by how many inches.
- **The partner sitting out** (ADR-042): in a singles ladder match, your
  opponent's doubles partner watches from the stands and cheers their
  teammate's points.
- **Fixes:**
  - In doubles, the GYM BROS (and any slow-reacting team) no longer let
    serves bounce past them: a CPU covering its lane reacted four times
    too slowly.
  - Your CPU partner no longer runs out past the corner to hit balls that
    have already gone out, most of all on the third shot.
- **Balance:** every rung of the ladder is harder than the one before, in
  singles and doubles. GYM BROS' BRAD is steadier but slower: a better
  player now beats him more often, instead of the match being a coin flip
  of balls hit out.
- **Saves:** unchanged from v0.11.0 (v0.4.0 and later saves carry over).
