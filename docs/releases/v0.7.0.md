Everything since v0.6.0:

- **Open play** (ADR-034): a new OPEN PLAY entry on the title takes you to
  a park with three courts, a game going on each.
  - The courts are skill levels: **<3.5** (teal, an easy game),
    **3.5-4.5** (gold, medium), and **4.5+** (red, hard). The ladder's
    teams play at their level.
  - Walk the park and press B at a court's rack to put your paddle in its
    stack. Walk past a court to see who's playing.
  - When a game ends, everyone comes off and the next paddles go on;
    players walk off and on, and a crowd watches from the benches.
  - When it's your turn: **YOUR GAME!** The captain of the team waiting
    next partners you in a doubles game against the team you were drawn
    with, at that court's level. Back to the park afterwards.
  - Two players: press Start on controller 2 and 2P comes along as your
    partner.
- **Fixes:** a steadier frame rate on the COURT page and when scrolling
  the title menu.
- **Saves:** unchanged from v0.6.0 (v0.4.0 and v0.5.0 saves carry over).
