Everything since v0.23.1:

- **Steadier doubles starts:** a CPU covering its half no longer takes a
  stray step in the first moments of a doubles match.
- **Smoother point results:** when the losing side has something to say
  after a point, the doubles action no longer hitches as the line appears.
- **Nothing about how the game plays has changed:** the opponents, the
  ladder, the menus, and your records work exactly as before. A lot of
  tidying happened behind the scenes.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
