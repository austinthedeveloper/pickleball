Everything since v0.23.0:

- **Smoother doubles:** the busiest doubles moments (all four players
  chasing a ball, with FAST BALL, POPCORN, or ICE on) no longer stutter,
  and starting a rematch is smoother too. The score and result panels
  now wait a moment when the game is busy, so the play itself never has
  to.
- **Nothing about how the game plays has changed:** the opponents, the
  ladder, and your records work exactly as before.
- **Your save** carries over as it is.
