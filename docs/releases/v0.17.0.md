Everything since v0.16.0:

- **Gear** (M4-CAREER-04): a career player's hat now changes their paddle
  stats, always as a trade-off. Each hat moves one point from one stat to
  another, and no stat goes past 5. The editor and Player Select show the
  stats with the hat on.
- **Quips** (M4-FUN-01): now and then, the side that lost a point says
  something (PG). Every ladder team has its own lines. It happens on about
  one lost point in three and never repeats a line twice in a row.
- **BALL SPEED** (M4-OPT-03): a new row on the OPTIONS page sets the ball
  from 70% to 120% speed, in 5% steps. The default stays 100%.
- **Fixes:**
  - Player Select shows your career player's hat.
  - The park's walk-around sprite wears your player's look.
  - At NIGHT, the partner watching a singles match from the stands is
    darkened like the crowd.
  - On a BLACK court, the ball's shadow is visible again.
- **Under the hood:** doubles matches and the menus have more frame time
  to spare. The CPUs' thinking and the player drawing are cheaper, and the
  scoreboard uploads faster. This makes room for better character models
  next. Play is unchanged.
- **Cartridge:** the ROM is now 1 MiB (it was 512 KiB). Flash carts
  (FXPak/SD2SNES, EverDrive) handle it as before. A reproduction board
  needs 1 MiB of 120 ns ROM.
