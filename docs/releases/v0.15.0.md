Everything since v0.14.0:

- **Charged serve** (M4-SERVE-01): a quick press still serves safely. Hold
  the button instead and a power bar fills and drains; let go for a faster
  serve the fuller it is, but let go as it drains to the bottom and the
  serve goes into the net. KID serves as before.
- **Night courts** (M4-COURT-03): a TIME row on the COURT page (DAY, DUSK,
  NIGHT) for any venue. At night the court plays under the lights and the
  crickets come out.
- **Three new venues** (M4-VENUE-04): DINOSAUR, SPACE, and UNDERSEA, each
  with its own background sounds and court colours. GYM BROS, GOTH CLUB,
  and SURF CREW now play at home there.
- **More court colours:** PINK, ORANGE, BLACK, and AQUA courts and
  kitchens; SNOW, CANDY, and AUTUMN surrounds; CYAN and LIME lines.
- **Fixes:**
  - The title's demo matches no longer end on the record initials screen
    (which then kept coming back after you entered initials).
  - A new best win streak now asks for your initials.
  - Players stand behind the baseline to serve, not in the court.
  - A player can no longer hit the ball their own partner just hit.
  - CPUs let balls that are landing out go, and KID's swing help only
    swings at a ball in play.
  - Doubles with all four modifiers no longer drops a frame as a point
    ends.
- **Known issue:** on a BLACK court the ball's shadow is hard to see.
- **Saves:** your records, settings, and initials carry over (v0.4.0 and
  later saves), in a new layout with room for the court's time.
