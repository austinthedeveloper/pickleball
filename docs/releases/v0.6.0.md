Everything since v0.5.0:

- **Modifiers** (ADR-033): a new MODIFIERS page on the title.
  - **BALL: BIG**, a bigger ball that is easier to reach.
  - **SPEED: FAST**, a quicker ball (on KID, the normal speed).
  - **SCORING: WIN BY 2**: at 5-5 in a game to 5, play on until someone
    leads by two.
  - **KITCHEN: LAVA**: step into your own kitchen during a rally and you
    lose the point. The kitchen glows while you choose it, and the CPUs
    know to stay out.
  - They combine, they're saved, and BIG and FAST work in practice too.
- **Smoother doubles:** the ball no longer stutters as it nears the
  players. A doubles match against the CPUs now runs without dropped frames
  (it dropped about one in 140), and the doubles title demo drops fewer
  than one in 500 instead of one in 30.
- **Fixes:**
  - the title menu is centred
  - returning to the title no longer sometimes drops a frame
- **Saves:** v0.4.0 and v0.5.0 saves carry over, records and settings
  included (ADR-032); the modifiers start off.
