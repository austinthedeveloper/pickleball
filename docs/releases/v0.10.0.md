Everything since v0.9.0:

- **Opponent portraits** (M4-STORY-03): a ladder match's intro card now
  shows the opponent captain's head and shoulders, framed, in their own
  colours. Doubles shows the team's captain.
- **Smoother doubles:** no dropped frames in doubles anymore, even with all
  four modifiers on, in the title demo, or scrolling the menus over it.
- **Faster menus:** the records page fills in a few frames instead of
  about a quarter second.
- **Saves:** unchanged from v0.9.0 (v0.4.0 and later saves carry over).
