Everything since v0.19.0:

- **Slice (X):** a slow shot that gives you time to get back in position.
  It never goes deep, and it stays steady when you have to stretch for
  the ball. The catch: it doesn't push anyone back.
- **Hit short with L or R:** hold either shoulder button and press a
  swing button to drop the ball short, while the Control Pad keeps moving
  you and aiming at a sideline. L+B is a drop, L+Y a dink, L+X a short
  slice, and **L+A a fast drop**: quick and low, but stretch for it and it
  sails long, right where they want it. Pressing away from the net still
  works too.
- **The CPUs slice:** the Goth Club and the Champs mix slices into their
  rallies (the Goth Club got a little tougher).
- **Two ladders:** singles and doubles now each remember your place.
  **Quitting a ladder match** from the pause menu (MAIN MENU) gives up
  your climb, and that ladder starts over with the first team. Losing
  still keeps your place. The records page shows both (S and D).
- **Fixed:** after quitting a doubles match, the title kept the match's
  VS panel under the scoreboard.
- **Your save** moves to a new layout the first time you start this
  version. Everything carries over, and both ladders start where your one
  ladder was.
