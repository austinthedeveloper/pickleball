# Pickleball '94 — Player Guide

One player against the CPU (singles), or with a CPU partner against two CPUs
(doubles), on a single outdoor court. A second player can join on
controller 2. First to 5 points wins.

On the title screen, Up/Down chooses **SINGLES** or **DOUBLES**. Pressing
Start on controller 2 adds a second player (`2P JOINED!`); press it again to
leave. Start or B on controller 1 opens Player Select.

## Player Select

Up/Down moves between the three rows (MALE/FEMALE, colour, paddle);
Left/Right changes the choice. B moves to the next row and, on the paddle
row, starts the match. Start confirms everything at once. Y steps back a
row, and from the top row returns to the title.

- **MALE / FEMALE**: the female player wears a ponytail.
- **Colour**: BLUE, RED, GREEN, or GOLD. With two players, 1P chooses
  first and 2P cannot take 1P's colour (it shows as a dark silhouette).
- **Paddle**: each paddle has the same total of stat points.

| Paddle | Power | Control | Speed | Plays like |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Wood | 2 | 5 | 2 | Late or stretched swings stay closer to the target. |
| Blue | 3 | 3 | 3 | Standard; the CPU uses it. |
| Red | 5 | 2 | 2 | Faster shots; stretched swings spray further. |
| Gold | 2 | 2 | 5 | Recovers sooner after a swing and moves faster while swinging. |

2P plays the opponent on the right side; in doubles each human gets a CPU
partner. The CPUs take the remaining colours.

## Controls

| Button | Action |
|---|---|
| D-pad | Move. At contact, it also aims the shot. |
| B | Standard swing. When you're holding the ball, B (or A/Y) serves. |
| A | Drive: a faster, flatter shot. Mistimed drives spray wider. |
| Y | Lob: a slow, high shot deep over an opponent at the net. With the D-pad away from the net, it's a dink that lands softly in the opponent's kitchen. |
| Start | Open Player Select from the title; pause and unpause during play; back to the title from the result screen. |
| Select | Debug and hardware-test ROMs only: OBJ stress scene. |

**Aiming at contact:** Up/Down sends the ball toward that sideline. Toward the net (Right) hits deep. Away from the net (Left) hits short, a drop shot near the kitchen.

**Timing:** a swing hits on its first frame with the ball in reach. Meet the ball with it close to your body and the shot goes where you aimed. Reach for it at the edge and it sprays wide and long, often out. Press B a moment before the ball arrives, not when it is far away.

## Arcade rules

These are simplified rules; the HUD says **ARCADE RULES**.

- Rally scoring: every rally scores a point, and the point winner serves next.
- First to 5 points wins. On the result screen, B rematches with the same
  players and Start returns to the title.
- Serve diagonally into the opponent's service box. Landing in the kitchen, in the wrong box, or out loses the point, and so does hitting the net.
- Two-bounce rule: the serve must bounce before it's returned, and the return must bounce before the server's side hits it. After that, volleys are allowed.
- A ball that lands out, hits the net, or bounces twice on a side ends the rally against the responsible side.
- Lines are in.
- Kitchen foot faults are not called.
- Doubles: each player covers a lane (you play the bottom half of your side,
  your partner the top). Your CPU partner leaves balls in your lane to you;
  the opponents spread their shots across both lanes. The serving side alternates which partner serves
  with its score, always diagonally, and teammates never swap sides.

## Result banners

| Banner | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OUT! | The shot landed outside the court. |
| NET! | The shot didn't clear the net. |
| KITCHEN! | The serve landed in the non-volley zone. |
| FAULT! | The serve landed in the wrong service box. |
| 2 BOUNCES | The receiving side failed to return the ball. |
| VOLLEY! | The ball was hit before a required bounce. |
